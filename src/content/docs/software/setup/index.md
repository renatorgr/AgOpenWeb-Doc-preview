AgOpenWeb steers and switches sections by the measurements you give it. A
tractor that weaves or a sprayer that switches late is most often a wrong
measurement, not a fault. Take a tape measure and do this once, carefully.

A good order to work in:

1. [Profiles](/software/setup/profiles/): make a profile for the tractor and
   one for the tool.
2. [Steer Wizard](/software/setup/steer-wizard/): it asks for the tractor's
   main measurements and calibrates the steering, step by step.
3. [Vehicle](/software/setup/vehicle/): the measurements the wizard does not
   ask for, such as the hitch.
4. [Tool](/software/setup/tool/): the kind of tool, its width and its
   sections.
5. [Machine module](/software/setup/machine-module/): only if you have one,
   for section valves and the hydraulic lift.

[AutoSteer settings](/software/setup/autosteer/) and
[Smart WAS](/software/setup/smart-was/) are for fine tuning afterwards.

Most settings carry the names they have in AgOpenGPS and mean the same. If you
copied your AgOpenGPS profiles across, they are already here: see
[Moving from AgOpenGPS](/software/moving-from-agopengps/). Check them once
before you rely on them.

:::tip
You can try any of this with the [simulator](/software/simulator/) first. The
simulator never steers the real tractor.
:::
