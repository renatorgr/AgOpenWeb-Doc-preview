On Android, AgOpenWeb is one app: the host and the screen together, on the
tablet. It needs **Android 6.0 or newer** and a tablet of
[10 inches or more](/software/what-you-need/).

The app is not in the Play Store. You install it from the file, which Android
calls sideloading.

1. On the tablet, download `agopenweb-android.apk` from the
   [releases page](https://github.com/AgOpenGPS-Official/AgOpenWeb/releases).
2. Open the downloaded file. Android asks whether the app you opened it from
   (your browser or file manager) may install apps. Allow it.
3. Install, then open **AgOpenWeb**. The guidance screen fills the display.
4. On the first start, allow **notifications**.

## It keeps running in the background

AgOpenWeb keeps guiding while you switch to another app or the screen turns
off, and Android requires a permanent notification for an app that does that.

It also keeps running when you swipe it away from the recent apps list. To stop
it:

- press **Back** on the guidance screen and confirm **Exit AgOpenWeb?**, or
- open Android's settings for the app and choose **Force stop**.

## The modules still need a cable

A tablet reaches the modules through a USB Ethernet adapter, not over Wi-Fi:
see [What you need](/software/what-you-need/#an-ethernet-cable-to-the-modules).

## Updating

Download the new `.apk` and install it over the old one. Your fields, profiles
and settings stay.
